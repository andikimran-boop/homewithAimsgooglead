Questions

Before you start

We're not ready to sell yet. Can we still ask?

Yes. Many families get the plan 6 to 12 months early, so they know their numbers before they decide.

Do we have to move out before we find our next home?

No. We plan your sale and purchase dates together. If needed, we arrange an extension of stay so you live in your flat until your new home is ready, if the buyer agrees.

Should we apply for another BTO near our parents instead?

You can, but as second-timers you'd wait years for it, need somewhere to stay meanwhile, and may pay a resale levy. The Proximity Housing Grant is only for resale flats, and resale lets you pick a home right near your parents.

Are we still eligible for grants and an HDB loan after selling our BTO?

Often, yes. The Proximity Housing Grant is open to second-timers who haven't received it before. Your plan checks this, and we help you confirm it with an HFE letter from HDB.

What happens to the CPF we used for our BTO?

When you sell, the CPF you used, plus interest, goes back into your CPF account. You can use it again for your next home. Your plan shows how much goes back to CPF and how much comes back to you as cash.

Should we upgrade now or wait a few more years?

It depends on your numbers, your kids' ages and prices near your parents. Your plan compares both, so you decide with facts, not fear.

Is the plan really free?

Yes, with no obligation. If you later decide to sell with us, we'll explain our fees clearly before you sign anything.
